# Siri AI in the EU

The European Union already delayed the Apple Intelligence in the iOS 26 update, we EU users only got it in ios 26.4. But this is a whole different story, now they don't even know if they will be able to ship us the AI. According to the DMA (Digital Markets Act) requires Apple to give **any AI system nearly unlimited access** to the user's device, as well as the **ability to act on that access autonomously without the user's ongoing visibility and control.**
Apple said that they will continue working with the EU to bring these, but there is no warranty that they will be able to provide us these features. The EU should accept these offers that Apple proposes because Apple want to keep these private, and the EU? No here they give no privacy to the user.
